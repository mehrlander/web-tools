# Cautions

These cautions cover risks we could miss when AI moves quickly.

## People's names

A person's name written into a repo can reach readers the person never expected, since a repo's files feed pages, artifacts and other repos, private repos included. Where the name came from decides whether it is already public. A name in a document published online is public, and the document can be shown or quoted with its names as published. A name that reached the session from the owner, a work email, a meeting, a chat or a session record usually is not, and the person's role often makes the point as well as the name would.

## web-tools is public

Everything written to web-tools is public: files, commit messages, branch names, pull request bodies and comments. Deleting something later does not reliably take it back, since copies can persist in forks, caches and search indexes. Material from a private repo, a session record or the owner's work becomes public when it lands there, and so does anything derived from it, such as a summary, a quotation or a figure. A convention or a method learned from private material carries none of the material itself.

## Drafts the owner will send

In a draft the owner will send at work, each sentence becomes the owner's statement to its recipient. An inference, an interpretation or a figure the session supplies becomes a claim the owner has to stand behind; raised beside the draft instead, it stays a suggestion the owner can weigh. The recipient also hears tone the session may not have meant: a sentence can sound pushy, undercut the request, or question a colleague's work.

## Prompts for another session

A prompt for another session gives that session the aim, the context and the intuition behind them, and the session brings its own judgment to the material. Procedure such as reading orders, samples and counts, a prescribed solution, or a ranking of the options replaces that judgment with a guess made before the material was read. A list of decisions to bring to the owner does the same for what the owner gets to see.
