# Writing Sprint

Punch up a text: restate its core insight using a move, a technique shown in a named writer's passage. Borrow the technique, not the writer's mannerisms or an assistant's stock phrasing.

1. State the text's core insight in one sentence.
2. Choose two or three moves that fit the insight from home's [`projects/skill-lab/moves.md`](https://github.com/mehrlander/home/blob/main/projects/skill-lab/moves.md). To channel a writer the file lacks, add their passage there first. The file's head says how.
3. Rewrite the insight sentence once per move. Leave out what that move's entry says to leave behind. Show the rewrites, one line each. Keep the strongest and say why.
4. Rebuild the text from that sentence. Open the new version with it. Add only the context a reader needs to follow it. [The Art of Arriving Together](https://github.com/mehrlander/home/blob/main/chron/2026/03/2026-03-10-the-art-of-arriving-together.md) says what that context is. To rebuild a prompt, [inspire-excellence](../inspire-excellence/SKILL.md) has forms for one.
