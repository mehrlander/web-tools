# Writing Sprint

Punch up a text by channeling writers who did this kind of writing well, in place of an assistant's stock phrasing.

1. State the text's core insight in one sentence.
2. Choose two or three writers to channel. Read a passage by each before writing: a piece in home's [exemplar set](https://github.com/mehrlander/home/tree/main/projects/skill-lab/exemplars), or one found for the job. A writer recalled from memory comes out as your own habits.
3. Rewrite the insight sentence once in each writer's manner. Show the rewrites, one line each. Keep the strongest and say why.
4. Rebuild the text from that sentence. Open the new version with it. Add only the context a reader needs to follow it. [The Art of Arriving Together](https://github.com/mehrlander/home/blob/main/chron/2026/03/2026-03-10-the-art-of-arriving-together.md) says what that context is. To rebuild a prompt, [inspire-excellence](../inspire-excellence/SKILL.md) has forms for one.
