# Research

web-tools holds no research accounts. They live in [web-tools-private `research/`](https://github.com/mehrlander/web-tools-private/tree/main/research), because most research reads material from private repositories, and a public repository cannot hold that material. web-tools keeps the conventions research follows: [`docs/probes.md`](../docs/probes.md) for probes and [`docs/source-bundles.md`](../docs/source-bundles.md) for material taken in from outside.

The Map's [Probes](https://mehrlander.github.io/web-tools/app/?view=map&tab=research) and [Exploration](https://mehrlander.github.io/web-tools/app/?view=map&tab=exploration) tabs list the accounts every repository declares under `research` in its `.web-tools.json`. The placement rule is in [Constellation architecture](../docs/CONSTELLATION.md#projects-and-research).
