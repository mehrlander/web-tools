# wacares-mps1: model

Source: Jerry Cornfield, "Voters to decide if WA Cares tax dollars can be
invested in stock market," Washington State Standard, Oct 31, 2025
(CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). Relative dates in the source ("this year", "summer",
"January", "Tuesday", "Friday") are read against that publication date.

## 1. Governing question and root

**Governing question:** "What am I deciding when I vote on SJR 8201?"

**Root:** the measure, Senate Joint Resolution 8201.

Judgment about the reader: a Washington voter who meets SJR 8201 as the first
item on the ballot. What they need first is what a yes vote changes; after
that, the two sides' cases, which they weigh against each other. WA Cares, its
money, the union paying for the yes campaign and the failed 2020 attempt are
things the measure points to, so they are neighbors, not the root. The root is
a single thing, not a set, so its full view is the measure's own facts; the
two comparisons inside it (today against if approved, yes against no) are its
parts.

### Form 2 and Form 3 decisions

- The balance belongs to the trust account, not to WA Cares or the measure (F41).
- The money facts belong to SEIU 775 (F47, F48); the yes side holds a reference to the union.
- "No funded opposition" is two facts from one sentence: one on the no side (F32), one on the 2020 measure (F45).
- The earnings difference between the two measures lives on the 2020 measure (F44); SJR 8201 holds its own earnings rule (F5 to F7) and a reference to the 2020 measure.
- The constitutional bar's facts (F14 to F17) are the "Today" member of the rule-state comparison, which is a part of the measure: the question is about the change, and the bar has no role on the page except as the before state.

Mechanisms: comparisons are two columns with each field on the same row;
references are labels that open one dialog, which follows the same rule (labels
inside it open the next view, with a back control naming where it returns);
the WA Cares milestones are a proportional timeline; evidence is a disclosure
on the claim it backs.

## 4. Checks

1. **No duplicates.** Each fact above has one home. Copies appear only in
   labels: the payroll tax rate and the trust account balance in the WA Cares label (bears on the
   question: the money at stake), SEIU 775's $2.5 million in its label in the
   money row (bears on the question: who pays for each side), and the 2020
   outcome in its label. "Billions of taxpayer dollars" (F3) is the measure's
   own phrase, not a copy of F41. Passed.
2. **Nothing lost.** Every fact F1 to F48 is reachable. Not carried as
   separate facts: the lede's "A similar attempt failed in 2020" and "managed
   like the state's pension funds," which are restatements of F42 and F19 and
   are folded into them; the byline, date, URL and license are in the source
   credit. Decision: no fact dropped.
3. **Scent.** Controls: the WA Cares, 2020 attempt and SEIU 775 labels name the
   thing they open and carry a qualifier; quotation disclosures read
   "Weiner's words" or "Hasegawa's words"; the dialog's back control names the
   view it returns to; the close control reads "Close". No "More" or
   "Details". Passed.
4. **Depth.** The answer (what a yes vote does, and what it takes to pass) is
   the first block on the page. Distances: rule states and sides, 0 (in the
   root view); WA Cares, its trust account and milestones, 2020 attempt, SEIU
   775, 1 interaction; SEIU 775 via the 2020 view or 2020 via SEIU, 2.
   Quotations are sources. Passed.
5. **No empty slots.** No side views (the row is the whole side); no view for
   the trust account (a part inside WA Cares), the board, people or the debate;
   no "Today" cell for the earnings rule, which the source does not describe
   for the current account, so the rule stands as its own block under the
   comparison instead of an empty cell. Passed.

## Build notes

The page keeps this model as its data: one `MODEL` object holding the root
(its own facts, the two comparisons, the background references) and a `views`
map for the three neighbors that earned a view (WA Cares, the 2020 attempt,
SEIU 775). Labels are generated from a view's name plus the qualifiers chosen
at each place. Rendered headless at 1280 and 390 pixels wide, light and dark:
no horizontal overflow, no console errors, the answer and identity block fit
the first phone screen, and the dialog's back control reads "Back to 2020
attempt" after following SEIU 775 from the 2020 view.
