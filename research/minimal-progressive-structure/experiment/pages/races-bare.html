Legislative races to watch as Washington’s election season revs up

With candidate filing closed, here’s an early look at some of the districts that might flip and where progressives are challenging other Democrats from the left.

From the Washington State Standard, May 8, 2026. The first of two installments looking at this year’s legislative races.

What’s at stake

All 98 seats in the state House and 24 of 49 state Senate seats are up for grabs. Progressives are trying to unseat more moderate Democrats, other Democrats are trying to take seats from Republicans to grow their majorities, and Republicans are trying to claw back any seats they can. With a slew of retirements, many new faces will arrive in Olympia next January.

Current majorities. Across the state, over 1,000 candidates are running for 600-plus federal, state and local offices.
