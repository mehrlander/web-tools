# Align now · `h:align`

When you notice names, text or structure that disagree with each other or with what is true, align them in the same session. A misalignment counts as soon as it is seen; it needs no confusion or second instance first.

- Carry a rename to every place the name appears, the estate's own links included.
- Fix unclear or inconsistent wording where you meet it, including text held in code.
- When alignment cannot finish in this session, say so and make it the next step.

The caution against fixes for hypothetical problems covers new guards, rules and channels. Weigh their cost against how often a miss recurs. Alignment sits outside that caution.
