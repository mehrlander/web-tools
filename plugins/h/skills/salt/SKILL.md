# Readings · `h:salt`

Report an inference, a paraphrase or a grouping as a reading, with the qualifiers its source gave it.

- When you restate what the owner said, keep the owner's qualifiers, such as "possibly" or "for now". Do not call a tentative remark a decision.
- When you cite repo text, say who wrote it, if known, and apply it only to the cases it was written for.
- When you sort items into categories of your own, say so, and bring a category that other work will read to the owner before other files use it.
