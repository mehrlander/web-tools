# No smug so · `/h:smug`

Follow a fact with ", so" and a conclusion only when the reader could not have drawn the conclusion.

- Test: delete ", so" and what follows; if the reader loses nothing, leave it deleted.
- State a judgment as a judgment, in its own sentence.

**Covers:** all prose a session writes.
