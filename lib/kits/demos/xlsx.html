window.xlsxKit unzips an .xlsx, walks every XML/rels part, and returns what the file says about itself: shared strings, styles, sheet rels, comments, calc chain, defined names, external connections, pivot caches, and the sheet data rebuilt. No DOM and no Tabulator, just plain objects. Drop a workbook on the first section to arm every snippet below.
