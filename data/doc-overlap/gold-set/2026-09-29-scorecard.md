# Restated lens: gold-set scorecard, 2026-09-29

This scores the claim behind the Themes tab's Restated lens: that a cosine
above the lens's cutoff marks two passages as restating each other. The program
under test is [`scripts/doc-overlap.py`](../../../scripts/doc-overlap.py) at the
commit that wrote [`matches.csv`](../matches.csv); the labels are
[`gold.csv`](gold.csv); the method is the portable `gold-set` skill.

## The run

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Universe | 545 unordered passage pairs at cosine 0.75 or more, from 184 Markdown files |
| Sample | 159 pairs in 19 strata (cosine band, shared shingles, passage under 20 words); seed 20260929; sample.csv md5 `9641b867501fb02ce2114cbc45700630` |
| Readers | 4 agents, the cheaper model, 40 pairs each; 300,485 tokens; 84 to 130 seconds each |
| Skeptics, first pass | 4 agents, the stronger model, 64 rows (disagreements with the 0.80 cutoff, every conflict and fragment, a seeded audit of 7 agreements); 390,236 tokens; 42 to 65 seconds each |
| Skeptics, full review | 4 agents, the same model, the other 95 rows; 427,165 tokens; 51 to 76 seconds each |
| Total | 12 agents, 1,117,886 tokens |

**Every row was reviewed, which departs from the skill's audit share.** The
first pass overturned 2 of the 7 audited agreements, one of them a pair at
cosine 1.000 sharing only the line "You receive these parameters in your
prompt:". Seven is too few to measure the readers' error on agreements, and
too many overturned to leave 95 labels resting on the readers alone, so the
rest were sent. The full review overturned 44 of 95. The final label on every
row is the skeptic's, and the reader's label stays beside it.

**The readers over-called `same`.** 25 of their `same` labels became
`summary` and 14 became `related`; the permitted `fragment` was overturned
11 times of 11 once the skeptic saw the surrounding text. The reader column is
evidence about the cheaper model, not about the documents.

## Reading

**No pair conflicts.** None of the 159, weighted to all 545, is a
contradiction; the one the readers called a conflict applies a single rule in
two places. The experiment's probe (approval against non-approval at 0.992) is
a real property of the model, but this corpus does not exhibit it. With 159
labels this bounds it, weakly; it does not exclude it.

**Cosine does not rank reading value.** About 62 percent of pairs are worth
reading side by side at every cutoff from 0.75 to 0.80, and the top band falls
to 42 percent because it is mostly vendored copies of the same file.

**Consolidation lives where the shingles already are.** Pairs whose files also
share 3+ shingles could be consolidated 60 percent of the time; pairs found by
embeddings alone, 9 percent. No cutoff makes a cosine a consolidation verdict:
even at 0.95 it is 79 percent.

**Short passages are the error.** Where the shorter passage is under 20 words,
44 percent of pairs are unrelated and 35 percent are worth reading; at 20 words
or more, 12 and 76. Every unrelated pair at cosine 1.000 is a short identical
line of boilerplate ("First match wins:", "Write a JSON file with this
structure:") introducing different content.

## Decision

**Change it, and not on this branch.** The program is scored where it is used,
and two changes follow; each needs a new sample to score, since tuning on these
labels and scoring on them would flatter the result.

1. Stop nominating a pair on a passage under 20 words alone, or weight such
   passages by how many files carry the same text (the experiment's proposed
   document-frequency filter). This is where the unrelated rows are.
2. Present the lens as related reading, not restatement, and start it at 0.75,
   where the reading share is as high as anywhere. "Restated" oversells a
   measure under which half the nominations restate.

Never read a cosine as a duplication verdict: consolidation needs the passages,
and the shingle lenses find most of what could be consolidated.
