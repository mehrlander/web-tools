# Related lens: held-out scorecard, 2026-09-29

This scores the Related lens as [PR #844](https://github.com/mehrlander/web-tools/pull/844)
set it, on pairs its settings were not tuned on. The
[first scorecard](2026-09-29-scorecard.md) drove three changes: hide pairs with
a passage under 20 words, start the lens at cosine 0.78, and mark matches from
0.85 as likely restating. It also predicted what they would show. From 0.78,
about four in five long pairs worth reading side by side and about 1 percent
unrelated. From 0.85, about nine in ten restating. Those predictions came from
the same labels that chose the settings, so this sample tests them.

## The run

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Universe | 268 unordered passage pairs at cosine 0.75 or more whose shorter passage is 20+ words, excluding the 91 such pairs the first sample labelled |
| Sample | 116 pairs, stratified as before; seed 20260930; sample.csv md5 `187382308faf743d3522123ca55c7589`; `sample --long-only --prefix h --n 120` |
| Readers | 3 agents, the cheaper model, 36 to 40 pairs each; 227,519 tokens |
| Skeptics | 3 agents, the stronger model, every row; 380,282 tokens; 91 to 104 seconds each |
| Total | 6 agents, 607,801 tokens |

Every row went to a skeptic, as in the first run, and 40 of 116 were
overturned. The readers again over-called `same`: 19 became `summary`.

## Reading

**The restatement mark holds.** From 0.85, 91 percent of pairs restate (same
or summary), against the 92 percent predicted.

**The starting cutoff was oversold.** From 0.78, 54 percent of pairs are worth
reading side by side, not about 80, and 9 percent are unrelated, not about 1.
Tuning and scoring on one set flattered it, which is what the held-out draw was
for.

**Part of the fall is the measure, not the lens.** "Worth reading" asks whether
seeing one passage beside the other helps the reader, and two copies of one
paragraph do not. So the measure falls as cosine rises, to 21 percent at 0.95,
while "could consolidate" rises to 95. Counting a pair as useful when it is
either worth reading or could be consolidated gives 86 percent from 0.78, 91
from 0.80 and 73 from 0.75.

| cutoff | shown (weighted) | unrelated | useful (reading or consolidate) | related |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.75 | ~268 | 17% | 73% | 42% |
| 0.78 | ~137 | 9% | 86% | 27% |
| 0.80 | ~104 | 6% | 91% | 21% |
| 0.82 | ~83 | 6% | 93% | 12% |
| 0.85 | ~58 | 5% | 93% | 3% |
| 0.90 | ~45 | 7% | 93% | 0% |

**The corpus now shows one conflict, and it is a stale document.**
`docs/environment/testing.md` line 96 says "The typography plugin is not
available (2026-08-01)" and tells a reader to vendor it by hand, while
`docs/SNAGS.md` near line 1492 records the fix: the package is installed under
an alias that `tools/render/cdn.mjs`'s `PKG_ALIAS` maps onto. The cosine is
0.781, just above the cutoff. The first run found no conflict among 159 pairs.
This one finding is what the lens exists to surface.

## Decision

**Keep 0.78 and 0.85, and correct the claim.** At 0.80 the unrelated share
would be 6 percent rather than 9, a difference of a few weighted pairs on a
sample this size, and choosing the cutoff from these labels would repeat the
flattery this run measured. The conflict above sits at 0.781 and would be
hidden at 0.80. The comment in `lib/alpineComponents/map.js` now states the
held-out figures in place of the first run's.
