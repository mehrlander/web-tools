# Diátaxis

A summary of [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/), Daniele Procida's framework for
technical documentation, as its site stated it on 2026-10-25. How the estate's
own kinds compare with it is in [doc-kinds.md](doc-kinds.md).

Diátaxis holds that documentation serves four distinct needs, so it takes four
forms, and that most documentation problems come from blurring them. It grew
from Procida's work on software product documentation at Divio, from 2014 to
2021, and has since been extended to scientific research. The site reports
adoption in hundreds of projects, with testimonials from Gatsby, Cloudflare and
Vonage.

## Two dimensions, four kinds

Two distinctions define the map. Content informs either **action** (practical
knowledge, "knowing *how*") or **cognition** (theoretical knowledge, "knowing
*that*"). And it serves a reader either at study, in the **acquisition** of a
skill, or at work, in its **application**. The site's compass reads the kind
off the two answers:

| Content informs | The reader is | Kind | Answers | Form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| action | acquiring a skill | **Tutorial** | "Can you teach me to…?" | a lesson |
| action | applying a skill | **How-to guide** | "How do I…?" | a series of steps |
| cognition | applying a skill | **Reference** | "What is…?" | dry description |
| cognition | acquiring a skill | **Explanation** | "Why…?" | discursive explanation |

The site's analogy is cooking: a tutorial is teaching a child to cook, a
how-to guide is a recipe, reference is the information on the back of a food
packet, and explanation is an article on culinary social history.

## What it asks of a writer

- **Keep the kinds apart.** "Crossing or blurring the boundaries described in
  the map is at the heart of a vast number of problems in documentation." The
  site's example is a tutorial overloaded with explanation. Its worst case is
  tutorials and how-to guides collapsing into one, which then meets neither
  need.
- **Reference is austere.** It describes the machinery "as succinctly as
  possible, and in an orderly way", follows the machinery's own structure, and
  links out to instruction and discussion rather than holding them.
- **Explanation may weigh views.** It is "a discursive treatment of a subject,
  that permits reflection", read away from the work, and it "can and must
  consider alternatives".
- **Improve in small steps.** Pick one improvement, make it, publish it, and
  repeat. Do not build empty sections for the four kinds in advance, and do not
  tear down and rebuild the whole: the structure emerges as the steps
  accumulate.
