Why the first quote was so large

The matching pair is docs/surfacing-course.md and skills/default/surfacing-course.md: the source document and a copy shipped with the default skill.

The duplication scan reports their shared text. Since almost the whole document matches, its first “passage” is almost the entire document. The number 832 counts matching ten-word sequences.

Atoms starts with the strongest match. Matrix also selects the strongest match before you choose a cell. That is why both views initially showed the same wall of text.

The quote provides evidence of the match. A match alone does not tell you that a copy is unnecessary: the shipped copy serves a delivery purpose.

This adjustment: keep a short excerpt in context and let “Show full passage” reveal the rest in the same place. The screenshot at left shows Matrix after scrolling to its evidence.

What the comparison exposes: the navigation still takes much of the phone screen, and Clusters repeats evidence for several pairs. Those are candidates for the broader integration pass.
