# Acronyms

This file collects, mostly for fun, the acronyms the owner has coined for asks made of a session and for an order of explaining a term. It is for the owner and for a session that meets one of the names.

## SPUR

Summary of Position for Unfamiliar Reader. Asked for a SPUR, summarize where the session stands, whether a proposal, a plan or progress so far, for someone who has not followed the session and knows none of the shorthand or coined terms the session uses.

## TOUR

Thorough Overview for Unfamiliar Reader. Asked for a TOUR, reassess the session's work from the reader's goal rather than from the session's current position: restate the goal, test whether the session's reasoning serves it, lay out the ways to reach the goal with the tradeoffs of each, and recommend one with its reason and the session's confidence. Write it for the reader of a [SPUR](#spur). A SPUR is the short ask and a TOUR the full one.

Names passed over for TOUR: FAIR, LEAF, SLATE, SLEDGE and DELVE.

## CDE

Concept, Definition, Examples: an order for explaining a term. A possible fourth letter is F, for Foil: the nearest case the term excludes, as in the [Frayer model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frayer_model).
