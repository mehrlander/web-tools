# Cautions

These cover moves whose harm the owner is unlikely to notice afterward. Each calls for judgment rather than a fixed rule, and names what to establish before acting. A repo's own narrower rule governs within its scope.

The canonical source is `mehrlander/web-tools` at `docs/CAUTIONS.md`. The portable plugin's `default` skill loads the byte-matched copy shipped beside it and fetches this source only as a fallback.

## People's names

Before writing a person's name into a repo, establish whether it is already public.

- A name in a document published online, shown or quoted as published, is fine. Do not strip names from such a document.
- A name that reached the session another way (from the owner, a work email, a meeting, a chat or a session record) is not public because it was mentioned. Leave it out, or name the role.
- A private repo does not settle the question: its files reach pages, artifacts and other repos.

## web-tools is public

Everything written to web-tools is public: files, commit messages, branch names, pull request bodies and comments.

- Before bringing material from a private repo, a session record or the owner's work into web-tools, derivatives included, establish that it is already public or that the owner wants it there.
- A convention or method learned from private material may go in.

## Drafts the owner will send

Text the owner sends at work becomes the owner's statement to its recipient.

- Keep to what the owner and the sources say. Raise an inference, an interpretation or a figure of your own beside the draft, not in it.
- Read each sentence as the recipient will: a line can sound pushy, undercut the request, or question a colleague's work.

## Prompts for another session

A prompt for another session gives the aim, the context and the intuition behind them, and leaves the approach to that session's judgment.

- Leave out procedure such as reading orders, samples and counts, and do not prescribe a solution or rank the options.
- Do not tell the session which decisions to bring to the owner.
